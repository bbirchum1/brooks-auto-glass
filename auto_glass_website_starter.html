How long does a rock chip repair take?

Most rock chip repairs take about 15–30 minutes depending on the size and location of the damage.

Can you come to my home or workplace?

Yes. Brooks Auto Glass is a mobile service, so we can often repair your windshield at your home, workplace, dealership, or fleet location.

How much does rock chip repair cost?

Mobile service is $50 for one rock chip. Additional rock chips repaired during the same visit receive a $15 discount each.

Will the repair completely remove the chip?

Every repair is different, but most repairs significantly improve the appearance of the damage and help stop it from spreading further.

Can a windshield chip spread if I wait?

Yes. Temperature changes, road vibration, and everyday driving can cause a small rock chip to spread into a larger crack over time.

Do you offer same-day appointments?

Yes. Same-day or next-day appointments are often available depending on scheduling and location.

What areas do you service?

We proudly serve Davis County, Salt Lake City, South Jordan, and many surrounding Utah communities.

Do you work on luxury vehicles and trucks?

Yes. We work on everyday vehicles, luxury vehicles, trucks, fleet vehicles, and commercial vehicles.

How many rock chips can be repaired?

That depends on the size, depth, and location of the damage. Contact us with photos and we can help determine the best option.
